# Research questions, predictions, and rival explanations

1. Under what measurable conditions does metabolic cross-feeding become stable and mutually beneficial across generations? **Rivals:** partner fidelity, kin assortment, environmental filtering, simple nutrient abundance.
2. When does increasing connectivity improve resilience, and when does it propagate collapse? **Rivals:** modularity, redundancy, diversity, network topology.
3. Can sequential resource use preserve more usable nourishment than concurrent use? Define resource quality and all affected organisms.
4. Can backward-reachability methods identify feasible constructive arrangements in ecosystems? Specify state variables, control limits, uncertainty and costs.
5. When does temporary coordination produce self-sustaining cooperation rather than dependence on a coordinator? Test withdrawal.
6. Can major evolutionary transitions be predicted from alignment of interests, bottlenecks, conflict suppression and feedback? Compare clonal and aggregative systems.
7. Does the observed history of complexity support a general trend, or selection/sampling biases and contingent expansions of a maximum? Include simplification and extinction.
8. Does a resource producer benefit from alternative decomposer distribution in any specific orchard? Distinguish saprotrophs from pathogens and pH effects from disease effects.
9. Are there cases where underlying organism needs are physically incompatible despite rearrangement? Treat genuine impossibility as valuable evidence.
10. How should flourishing, sentience, welfare, biodiversity, autonomy and future creative capacity be weighed when they conflict? This is partly normative and cannot be solved by evolutionary facts alone.

## First experiment proposal

Build a **toy ecological resource-flow model** with producer biomass, fruit yield, insect population and life stages, decomposer biomass, pathogen incidence, nutrient return, and tree health. Compare no intervention to alternative timing/location of resource access. Validate parameter ranges against primary literature; run sensitivity analyses; report where a purported constructive arrangement fails. Do not fit parameters to guarantee the desired answer.
